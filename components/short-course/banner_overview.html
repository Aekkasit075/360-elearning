About the Program

The Master of Business Administration in Business Innovation has been designed to develop the next generation of leaders for the digital era. The curriculum is built upon a modern approach to innovation-driven business management, equipping students with the knowledge, strategic mindset and practical skills required to succeed in today's rapidly evolving business landscape. The program covers essential areas including strategic planning, business management, innovation development, specialized business operations, and leadership. Students will gain the expertise to lead high-performing teams, drive organizational innovation, and create sustainable competitive advantages. Graduates will be prepared to manage and transform businesses with confidence, enabling organizations to compete successfully at both regional and international levels.
