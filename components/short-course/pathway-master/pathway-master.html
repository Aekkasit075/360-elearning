Pathway to a Master's Degree

1

Study online at:
www.SkillLane.com/TUXSA

2

Take online exams to earn and
collect credits.

3

Receive a Course Certificate
upon passing the online criteria
for each individual course.

4

Receive a Program Certificate
after passing all required
compulsory courses, totaling
18 credits (12 courses) via the
online platform.

5

Register and take the
Comprehensive Examination.

6

Apply for master's degree
student status and select a
study plan according to the
admission requirements of the
university and the hosting
faculty.

7

Plan 1: Complete 24 credits of online
coursework (16 courses) according
to the curriculum structure with a
GPA of not less than 3.00, pass the
Thesis, and achieve the required
English proficiency test score.

Plan 2: Complete 30 credits of online
coursework (20 courses) according
to the curriculum structure with a
GPA of not less than 3.00, pass the
Independent Study (IS), and achieve
the required English proficiency test
score.

8

Graduate with a Master's
Degree from Thammasat
University.
